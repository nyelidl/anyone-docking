Anyone Can Dock GPT

Molecular docking for everyone.

Resolve compound names, find PDB structures, choose an evidenced pocket, submit docking jobs, and inspect returned scores, poses, available contacts, and structure files.

Try a docking workflow

“Dock gefitinib against PDB 1M17 at pH 7.4 using the co-crystal ligand to define the pocket.”

The plugin uses the connected Anyone Can Dock service. It requires an internet connection. Docking scores and predicted poses are computational estimates; they do not establish experimental binding affinity or clinical effectiveness.

Privacy: the service has no account system. Anyone holding a job ID or result URL may access its results. Do not submit confidential or proprietary structures.
