Support

For general support, open an issue in Anyone Can Dock GitHub Issues. Include the error, requested settings, and steps to reproduce. Do not post private structures, credentials, or result URLs publicly.

For deletion and private requests, email kowith@ccs.tsukuba.ac.jp with the job ID.

Delete a finished job

Ask the plugin: Delete job [your actual job ID]. The MCP deleteJob tool uses DELETE /jobs/{job_id}. Queued and running jobs cannot be deleted through this endpoint.

Download important results promptly. Jobs may disappear on a server restart or redeployment.
